About Your Guide

Hi, I'm Jess.

A few years ago, I was deep in corporate life, checking boxes, climbing invisible ladders, and slowly losing touch with what I actually wanted. I was also diving headfirst into the online business world — building chatbots, taking courses, testing every new AI tool that landed in my feed.

From the outside? It probably looked like hustle. From the inside? It was fragmentation dressed up as productivity.

So I stopped. I shut it all down. And I started asking different questions: How do we stay ambitious without completely frying our nervous systems? How do we use AI as a tool for building something real — instead of just another source of overwhelm?

I'm not a guru who has it all figured out. I'm a certified life and health coach, a practitioner who's doing the work, and a woman who learned the hard way that busy is not the same as productive, and stimulated is not the same as satisfied.

Today I help midlife women navigate the AI-driven world without losing themselves in it. Not by teaching them every tool. By helping them find their lane, build with focus, and use AI as the vehicle — not the destination.

— Jess, Midlife Recalibrated
